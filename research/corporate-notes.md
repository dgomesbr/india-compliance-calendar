# Corporate law, securities and FEMA - research notes

Companion to `corporate.json` (55 rules: 29 MCA including 4 LLP, 11 SEBI, 15 RBI/FEMA). Checked on 2026-10-14. Every rule cites at least one primary source that was opened during this session. MCA documents are cited through the MCA e-book document server (`/bin/ebook/dms/getdocument?doc=...`), because the "What's New" download links (`/bin/dms/getdocument?mds=...`) redirect non-browser clients to the home page. The RBI Master Direction on reporting PDF sits behind a bot challenge, so RBI rules cite the HTML regulation and circular pages instead, which carry the same text.

## Leads from the reference site

**DIR-3 KYC triennial regime - confirmed.** G.S.R. 943(E) dated 31-12-2025, in force 31-03-2026, substituted Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules 2014. Every DIN holder as on 31 March files DIR-3-KYC-Web "on or before the 30th June of the immediately following every third consecutive financial year", and any change in personal mobile number, email or residential address must be filed within 30 days of the change. G.S.R. 300(E) dated 21-04-2026 set the fees: nil within the timeline, Rs 5,000 for late filing or DIN reactivation, Rs 500 for every change filing. MCA's DIR-3-KYC-Web FAQ fixes the first cycle: DINs allotted on or before 31-03-2025 (KYC already filed in 2025) file next between April and June 2028; DINs allotted during FY 2025-26 file first between April and June 2029. The last annual round (due 30-09-2025) was extended without fee to 15-10-2025 (GC 04/2025) and 31-10-2025 (GC 05/2025).

**General Circular No. 02/2026 on DPT-3 - confirmed.** The circular (dated "June 2026", listed by MCA on 19-06-2026) allows DPT-3 for FY 2025-26 to be filed without additional fees up to 31-07-2026 because of restoration work at the MCA data centre after a fire on 05-06-2026. It is a fee waiver, not an amendment of Rule 16, and is recorded as an override on `mca-dpt3-annual`.

**CSR-2 as an addendum linked to AOC-4 - confirmed, with a correction.** G.S.R. 357(E) dated 30-05-2025 is the Companies (Accounts) *Second* Amendment Rules 2025 (in force 14-07-2025); it substituted the V3 AOC-4 family and CSR-2, and the new CSR-2 asks for the SRN of the AOC-4. The separate-filing deadlines in Rule 12(1B) stop at FY 2023-24, whose date G.S.R. 317(E) dated 19-05-2025 (the first Amendment Rules) moved to 30-06-2025. From FY 2024-25 onward CSR-2 follows the AOC-4 due date.

## Modelling assumptions

AGM-linked rules are annual rules that assume the AGM is held on its last permitted day, 30 September: AOC-4 30 October, MGT-15 30 October, MGT-7/MGT-7A 29 November, ADT-1 14 October, CSR-2 with AOC-4. The real deadline runs from the actual AGM date. ADT-1 uses 14 October because it counts the AGM day as day 1; counting from the next day (General Clauses Act style) gives 15 October, so 14 October is the safe date. ADT-1 is only filed in years when an auditor is appointed or re-appointed.

The annual report and BRSR (Reg 34) are due when dispatch to shareholders begins; 8 September is the latest electronic dispatch date for a 30 September AGM with 21 clear days' notice. Top 100 entities, whose AGM must be held by 31 August, will dispatch earlier.

CRA-2 is modelled as 180 days after the end of the preceding financial year (27 September), which is the outer limit for appointing the cost auditor for the year that has just begun; the occurrence labelled FY X is therefore the CRA-2 for FY X+1. CRA-4 is modelled as 30 days after the latest permitted cost audit report date (27 October). Both are earlier if the Board acts earlier. ECB 2 is modelled as a monthly reminder on the 7th, although since 16-02-2026 it is only due for months with a drawdown, debt servicing or other change in the outstanding.

## What changed in 2025-2026

On the MCA side the biggest change is the DIR-3 KYC move from an annual 30 September filing to a triennial 30 June filing plus event-based updates. Small-company limits were raised to paid-up capital of Rs 10 crore and turnover of Rs 100 crore (G.S.R. 880(E), 01-12-2025), which widens MGT-7A and narrows the private companies covered by Rule 9B demat and PAS-6. Rule 9B got a final window to 30-06-2025 for private companies that were not small on 31-03-2023 (G.S.R. 131(E)). All annual-filing, audit and cost-audit forms moved to MCA21 V3 on 14-07-2025 (G.S.R. 357/358/359/360/361(E)) without any change to due dates, and AOC-4 now carries linked extracts of the Board's report and auditor's report (new Rule 12(1C)).

MCA relief in the period mostly concerns years before this calendar's window: the V2-V3 transition (GC 01/2025, forms due 18-06 to 31-07-2025 could be filed up to 15-08-2025), FY 2024-25 annual filings without additional fee to 31-12-2025 and then 31-01-2026 (GC 06/2025, GC 08/2025), CRA-4 for FY 2024-25 to 31-12-2025 (GC 07/2025), and the Companies Compliance Facilitation Scheme 2026 (GC 01/2026; 15-04-2026 to 15-07-2026, extended to 31-08-2026 by GC 03/2026 and to 15-09-2026 by GC 04/2026), which let defaulting companies file old annual returns and financial statements with 10% of the additional fee. GC 03/2025 allows AGMs by video conference till further orders but grants no extra time. As of 14-10-2026 no relaxation has been issued for FY 2025-26 AGMs, AOC-4 or MGT-7; GC 04/2026 is the last circular on MCA's list.

On the SEBI side, Integrated Filing (SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-2/CIR/P/2024/185 dated 31-12-2024, restated in the LODR Master Circular of 30-01-2026) now carries most quarterly LODR filings. Integrated Filing (Governance) is due 30 days after every quarter end and subsumed the standalone Reg 27(2) corporate governance report and Reg 13(3) investor grievance statement (both previously due in 21 days); their old XBRL utilities stopped from the March 2025 quarter. Integrated Filing (Financial) is due 45 days after Q1-Q3 and 60 days after Q4, and contains the Reg 33 results, the Reg 32(1) statement of deviation, the disclosure of defaults on loans and debt securities (previously within 7 days of quarter end) and the half-yearly Reg 23(9) RPT disclosure; the standalone XBRLs stopped from 01-04-2025. The shareholding pattern (Reg 31, 21 days), secretarial compliance report (Reg 24A, 60 days), share capital audit (DP Reg 76, 30 days), the Reg 74(5) certificate and the annual report and BRSR remain separate. Reg 7(3) (annual share transfer compliance certificate) was omitted with effect from 13-12-2024, so it has no rule. The quarterly SDD compliance certificate was rescinded in October 2024: main-board entities confirm SDD compliance inside the Reg 24A report, and others file an annual certificate within 60 days. BRSR Core assessment or assurance follows the glide path in the SEBI circular of 28-03-2025 (top 500 for FY 2025-26, top 1,000 for FY 2026-27). LODR amendments in 2025 and 2026 (including the January 2026 change that raised the high value debt listed entity threshold to Rs 5,000 crore and the July 2026 Second Amendment on transfers) did not move any periodic due date. SOP fines in the 2026 Master Circular are unchanged.

On the FEMA side, RBI replaced the ECB framework through the FEM (Borrowing and Lending) (First Amendment) Regulations 2026 (FEMA 3(R)(5)/2026-RB dated 09-02-2026, published 16-02-2026). Form ECB and Revised Form ECB became Form ECB 1 and Revised Form ECB 1, and the monthly ECB-2 return due within 7 working days became Form ECB 2, due within 7 calendar days after a month with drawdown or debt servicing; the new reporting applies to existing ECBs too. A.P. (DIR) 25/2026 (from 01-04-2026) treats ECB 1 forms as non-flow returns and charges the fixed Rs 7,500 per late ECB 2 return. The new FEM (Guarantees) Regulations 2026 created a quarterly Form GRN return (15 days after quarter end). FDI, ODI and FLA timelines and the general late submission fee formula did not change.

## Open questions and lower-confidence items

The CSR-2 late fee is unclear: no CSR-2-specific additional-fee slab was found, so the exposure uses the Section 450 general penalty (confidence medium). PAS-6 for private companies under Rule 9B has no MCA guidance on which half-year the first return covers. MCA has not said how OPCs (which hold no AGM) count the 60 days for MGT-7A; 29 November is the common practice. The DIR-3 KYC cycle for DINs allotted from FY 2026-27 onward, and the fee for a change reported after 30 days, are not specified. NFRA-2 is the auditor's obligation; the rule date (30 November) is clear, but the penalty in Rule 13 is a small fine.

The Reg 74(5) quarterly certificate within 15 days of quarter end is exchange practice rather than written rule (confidence medium). SEBI earmarked the shareholding pattern for "system driven" disclosure by the exchanges, but no implementing exchange circular was found, so the 21-day filing stays. The first GRN quarter is uncertain because the files only came out on 01-04-2026; the rule starts from the January-March 2026 quarter. RBI has not said whether a nil ECB 2 is needed in months without transactions. The LLP BEN-2 penalty relies on Section 90 of the Companies Act applying to LLPs through the 2023 rules (confidence medium). Producer companies are said to have 60 days for AOC-4 under Section 378ZA; not verified here.

The Corporate Laws (Amendment) Bill 2026 (introduced 23-03-2026, Joint Committee report 03-08-2026, per PRS - a secondary source) is not law. If passed it would affect several rules here, including VC AGMs, a longer further window for CHG-1 for prescribed companies and a higher CSR net-profit trigger. MCA's circular list may lag by a few days, so anything issued between 5 and 14 October 2026 may be missing.

## Not included

No IFSCA or IBBI rules were added: no clearly periodic, company-level filing could be confirmed on a primary source within the time available. Form InVI (investment vehicles), Form DRR (custodians), LEC returns (AD banks) and Form GNL-1 (AGM extension application) were left out as niche or bank-side items. The new service-export EDF (monthly, from 01-10-2026, FEM (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations 2026) belongs with the trade research. LODR event disclosures (Reg 30, Reg 44(3) voting results) were not modelled.
